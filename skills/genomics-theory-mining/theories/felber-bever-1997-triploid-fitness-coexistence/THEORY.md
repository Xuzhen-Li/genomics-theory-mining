# Felber-Bever 1997 triploid-fitness coexistence

```
Theory: Felber-Bever 1997 effect of
  triploid fitness on diploid-
  tetraploid coexistence
Original paper (year): Felber F, Bever
  JD. 1997. Effect of triploid fitness
  on the coexistence of diploids and
  tetraploids. Biol J Linn Soc 60(1):
  95-106 (January 1997).
  DOI 10.1111/j.1095-8312.1997.tb01485.x
  (also 10.1006/bijl.1996.0090).
  Crossref works JSON opened
  (https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1111/j.1095-8312.1997.tb01485.x):
  Felber F; Bever JD; Biological

  journal-article (author, year,
  venue, pages). Claim text opened on

  REST: deterministic model; diploids
  may produce 2n gametes; triploids
  have lower fitness than other
  cytotypes and produce equal
  proportions of haploid and diploid
  gametes. When diploids produce only
  haploid gametes, the dynamics match
  heterozygote disadvantage, with
  triploids as the heterozygotes.
  Production of 2n gametes by diploids
  creates a stable equilibrium of a
  majority of diploids and a minority
  of polyploids. When tetraploid
  fitness is equal to or higher than
  diploid fitness, increasing triploid
  fitness lowers the 2n-gamete
  threshold that deterministically
  fixes tetraploids. When tetraploids
  are less fit, that threshold first
  falls and then rises with triploid
  fitness. Triploids are repeatedly
  found in 2x-4x hybridizations and
  are rarely totally sterile; they can
  decide the future of mixed-cytotype
  populations. OUP HTML Cloudflare;
  the lock is Crossref (author+year+
  venue) plus the DOI-landing claim.
  Distinct from Felber 1991 this wave
  (two-cytotype 2n threshold; not
  triploid fitness). Distinct from
  filled Husband 2000 (experimental
  tetraploid fitness, not the 1997
  triploid-fitness model). Distinct
  from Levin 1975 this wave. Husband
  2004 (triploid bridge in Chamerion)
  still has no opened abstract; 1997
  is the triploid-fitness lock. Distinct
  from filled Stebbins 1947.
One-sentence prediction: Raising
  triploid fitness can lower the 2n-
  gamete threshold that fixes
  tetraploids when tetraploid fitness
  is at least that of the diploid;
  triploids are not a dead end.
Assumptions: a sexual mixed 2x / 3x /
  4x population; Vitis is not that
  mix; grafted clones skip cytotype
  mating.
Which fail here: Vitis is palaeo-
  hexaploid, not a mixed 2x / 3x / 4x
  sexual population. Grafted clones
  skip the triploid-bridge walk. Dual
  domestication is two origins. A
  nursery hybrid is not that
  deterministic mix.
Modern data / statistic: whether Vitis
  ploidy after clone filters is a
  Felber-Bever triploid-fitness series
  (triploids lowering the 4x-fixation
  threshold), versus old hexaploidy or
  two origins.
Dataset: Jaillon et al. 2007 Nature
  449:463-467 (DOI 10.1038/nature06148).
  Three ancestral genomes; no recent
  WGD. Use as the triploid-fitness
  contrast, not as a published grape
  1997 analysis. Do not invent a grape
  triploid-bridge paper.
What would falsify it: Vitis ploidy is
  not a Felber-Bever triploid-fitness
  series after clone filter (old
  hexaploidy, or two origins). The 2n
  threshold remains Felber 1991.
  Minority exclusion remains Levin
  1975. Experimental fitness remains
  Husband 2000. Husband 2004 was not
  opened.
What we will not claim: that Felber
  1991 is this paper. That Husband 2000

  That VviAGL11 is the sex locus.
```
