# Gavrilets 1999 holey-landscape dynamics

```
Theory: Gavrilets 1999 dynamical theory of
  speciation on holey adaptive landscapes
Original paper (year): Gavrilets S. 1999.
  A dynamical theory of speciation on
  holey adaptive landscapes. Am Nat
  154(1):1-22 (1 July 1999).
  DOI 10.1086/303217 PMID 29587497.
  EuropePMC HTML opened
  (https://europepmc.org/article/MED/29587497):
  Gavrilets S; 01 Jul 1999; The American
  Naturalist 154(1):1-22; DOI; PMID;
  abstract. A holey adaptive landscape is
  one where relatively infrequent well-fit
  combinations of genes form a contiguous
  set that expands throughout genotype
  space. Models of the complete process of
  speciation from initiation until
  completion, driven by mutation and
  random genetic drift, with selection
  (reproductive isolation), drift,
  mutation, recombination, and migration.
  Pre- and postmating isolation is a
  consequence of cumulative genetic
  change. Allopatric, parapatric,
  peripatric, and vicariance scenarios.
  Rapid speciation, including simultaneous
  emergence of several new species, is a
  plausible outcome in subdivided
  populations, most likely when there are
  many small subpopulations. Speciation is
  possible even when subpopulations
  exchange several individuals per
  generation. Selection for local
  adaptation is not necessary for rapid
  speciation. Chicago HTML still
  Cloudflare; the lock is the EuropePMC
  abstract (author, year, venue, DOI, and
  the claim). Distinct from filled
  Gavrilets 1997 Evolution (two-locus
  Dobzhansky-ridge barrier to neutral
  gene flow; not the complete holey-
  landscape walk). Distinct from filled
  Gavrilets 2000 (parapatric waiting
  time). Distinct from filled Dobzhansky
  1937 BDM and filled Dobzhansky 1940
  reinforcement. Distinct from filled
  Barton 1979 / 1983 / 1986. Distinct
  from filled Orr 1995 snowball and
  filled Orr-Turelli 2001 wait. Gavrilets
  1997 TREE is a review (not used).
  Gavrilets 2004 is a book.
One-sentence prediction: Rapid speciation,
  including several new species at once,
  can finish on a holey adaptive
  landscape in a subdivided population
  without local adaptation and without
  crossing a deep valley.
Assumptions: a sexual population
  subdivided into many small demes
  walking a holey ridge; dual
  domestication is not that walk; a
  nursery hybrid is not that completion.
Which fail here: dual domestication is
  two origins, not a holey-ridge walk.
  Dong 2023 Syl-W gene flow is CAUTION,
  not a 1999 completion. Human transport
  is not the paper's few-migrant
  exchange. Nursery hybrids are not that
  cline. A cultivar panel is not many
  small demes.
Modern data / statistic: whether wild-
  cultivar or interspecific isolation
  after clone + IBD filters is a
  Gavrilets-1999 holey-landscape
  completion (rapid isolation in a
  subdivided population without local
  adaptation), versus two origins or one
  locus.
Dataset: Dong et al. 2023 Science
  (DOI 10.1126/science.add8655). Syl-W
  gene flow as CAUTION, not a 1999
  holey-landscape series. Use as the
  holey-landscape contrast, not as a
  published grape 1999 analysis. Do not
  invent a grape holey-ridge paper.
What would falsify it: isolation is not
  a 1999 holey-landscape completion
  after clone filter (flat, or two
  origins, or one locus). Two-locus ridge
  barrier remains Gavrilets 1997.
  Parapatric wait remains Gavrilets 2000.
  Hybrid-zone dynamics remain Barton
  1979. BDM remains Dobzhansky 1937.
What we will not claim: that Gavrilets
  1997 Evolution is this paper (already
  filled). That Gavrilets 2000 is this
  paper. That Gavrilets 1997 TREE is the
  source (review). That Dong 2023 mapped
  a holey ridge. That dual domestication
  is holey-landscape speciation. That
  VviAGL11 is the sex locus.
```
