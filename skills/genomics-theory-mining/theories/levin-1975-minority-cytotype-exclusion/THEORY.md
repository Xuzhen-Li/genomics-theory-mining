# Levin 1975 minority-cytotype exclusion

```
Theory: Levin 1975 minority-cytotype
  exclusion
Original paper (year): Levin DA. 1975.
  Minority cytotype exclusion in local
  plant populations. Taxon 24(1):35-43
  (February 1975).
  DOI 10.2307/1218997. Crossref works
  JSON opened
  (https://api.crossref.org/works/10.2307/1218997):
  Donald A. Levin, Department of Botany,
  University of Texas Austin; Taxon 24:
  35-43; February 1975; DOI; type
  journal-article; Summary. Random
  cross-pollination and incomplete self-
  pollination in two cytotypes that
  cannot hybridize: reproductive success
  is frequency-dependent; the minority
  is at a disadvantage because it
  suffers a greater proportion of
  ineffectual pollinations. That
  handicap grows, and the minority
  cytotype is rapidly excluded. Self-
  pollination and immigration of
  minority pollen or seeds slow the
  exclusion. Unless the two cytotypes
  have disparate ecological
  requirements, a minority disadvantage
  causes local extinction of one
  cytotype even if they are equally fit
  in all respects. Wiley HTML still
  Cloudflare; EuropePMC has no record
  with an abstract; the lock is the
  Crossref Summary (author, year,
  venue, DOI, and the claim). Distinct
  from filled Husband 2000 (the first
  experimental frequency-dependent
  fitness series in Chamerion; not the
  1975 model). Distinct from filled
  Stebbins 1947 types and filled
  Masterson 1994 stomatal census.
  Distinct from Felber 1991 this wave
  (2n-gamete threshold, not the
  minority-pollination model). Levin
  1983 Am Nat still has no claim text.
One-sentence prediction: In two
  cytotypes that cannot hybridize, the
  minority is rapidly excluded by
  ineffectual pollinations unless the
  cytotypes have disparate ecological
  requirements.
Assumptions: a sexual mixed-cytotype
  population with random cross-
  pollination; Vitis is not that 2x /
  4x mix; grafted clones skip cytotype
  mating.
Which fail here: Vitis is palaeo-
  hexaploid (Jaillon 2007), not a mixed
  2x / 4x sexual population. Grafted
  clones skip pollination between
  cytotypes. Dual domestication is two
  origins. A nursery block is not the
  paper's local population.
Modern data / statistic: whether Vitis
  ploidy after clone filters is a
  Levin-1975 minority-cytotype
  exclusion (frequency-dependent
  ineffectual pollination), versus old
  hexaploidy or two origins.
Dataset: Jaillon et al. 2007 Nature
  449:463-467 (DOI 10.1038/nature06148).
  Three ancestral genomes; no recent
  WGD. Dong et al. 2023 Science
  (DOI 10.1126/science.add8655) as the
  wild-versus-cultivar contrast after
  clone + IBD filters. Use as the
  minority-cytotype contrast, not as a
  published grape 1975 analysis. Do not
  invent a grape 2x / 4x exclusion
  paper.
What would falsify it: Vitis ploidy is
  not a Levin-1975 exclusion after
  clone filter (old hexaploidy, or two
  origins). Experimental fitness
  remains Husband 2000. Types remain
  Stebbins 1947. Stomatal census
  remains Masterson 1994.
What we will not claim: that Husband
  2000 is this paper (already filled).
  That Levin 1983 is the source (still
  no claim text). That Jaillon 2007
  measured minority pollination. That
  VviAGL11 is the sex locus.
```
