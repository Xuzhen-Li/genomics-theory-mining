# Felber 1991 2n-gamete threshold

```
Theory: Felber 1991 establishment of a
  tetraploid cytotype via diploid 2n
  gametes
Original paper (year): Felber F. 1991.
  Establishment of a tetraploid
  cytotype in a diploid population:
  Effect of relative fitness of the
  cytotypes. J Evol Biol 4(2):195-207
  (March 1991).
  DOI 10.1046/j.1420-9101.1991.4020195.x.
  Crossref works JSON opened
  (https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1046/j.1420-9101.1991.4020195.x):
  Felber F; Journal of Evolutionary
  Biology 4:195-207; March 1991; DOI;
  type journal-article; Abstract.
  Deterministic model: the diploid
  produces some 2n gametes. If fertility
  and viability of both cytotypes are
  the same and the initial population is
  diploid, a mixed population occurs if
  2n-gamete production is below 17.16
  percent; the tetraploid excludes the
  diploid above that limit. Doubling
  one of fertility or viability in the
  polyploid drops the threshold to 10
  percent; doubling both drops it to 6
  percent. Conditions for establishment
  are quite restrictive. Spread in
  nature would probably require
  immigration of polyploids, or a
  genetical or environmental change, or
  chance in small populations that
  substantially raises diploid 2n-
  gamete production. Wiley HTML still
  cookies-blocked; the lock is the
  Crossref Abstract. Distinct from
  Levin 1975 this wave (minority
  pollination, not the 2n threshold).
  Distinct from filled Husband 2000
  (experimental fitness, not the 17.16
  percent threshold). Distinct from
  Felber and Bever 1997 this wave
  (triploid fitness, not the two-
  cytotype 2n threshold). Distinct from
  filled Stebbins 1947 and filled
  Masterson 1994.
One-sentence prediction: A tetraploid
  established from diploid 2n gametes
  persists in mixture only below about
  17 percent 2n-gamete production when
  fitnesses are equal, and the
  tetraploid excludes the diploid above
  that threshold.
Assumptions: a sexual diploid
  population that leaks 2n gametes;
  Vitis is not that population; grafted
  clones skip cytotype mating.
Which fail here: Vitis is palaeo-
  hexaploid, not a diploid leaking 2n
  gametes. Grafted clones skip the
  establishment walk. Dual
  domestication is two origins. A
  nursery block is not a small
  population raising 2n-gamete
  frequency.
Modern data / statistic: whether Vitis
  polyploidy after clone filters is a
  Felber-1991 2n-gamete threshold
  (mixture below 17.16 percent; 4x
  exclusion above it), versus old
  hexaploidy or two origins.
Dataset: Jaillon et al. 2007 Nature
  449:463-467 (DOI 10.1038/nature06148).
  Three ancestral genomes; no recent
  WGD. Use as the 2n-threshold
  contrast, not as a published grape
  1991 analysis. Do not invent a grape
  2n-gamete paper.
What would falsify it: Vitis ploidy is
  not a Felber-1991 2n-threshold after
  clone filter (old hexaploidy, or two
  origins). Minority exclusion remains
  Levin 1975. Experimental fitness
  remains Husband 2000.
What we will not claim: that Levin 1975
  is this paper. That Husband 2000 is
  this paper. That Felber and Bever
  1997 is this paper. That Jaillon 2007
  estimated a 2n-gamete rate. That
  VviAGL11 is the sex locus.
```
