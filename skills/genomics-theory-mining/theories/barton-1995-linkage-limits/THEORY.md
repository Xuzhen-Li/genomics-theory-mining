# Barton 1995 linkage limits

```
Theory: Barton 1995 linkage limits on
  the fixation of weakly favored
  alleles
Original paper (year): Barton NH. 1995.
  Linkage and the limits to natural
  selection. Genetics 140(2):821-841
  (1 June 1995).
  DOI 10.1093/genetics/140.2.821
  PMID 7498757 PMCID PMC1206655.
  EuropePMC HTML opened
  (https://europepmc.org/article/MED/7498757):
  Barton NH, Institute of Cell, Animal
  and Population Biology, University of
  Edinburgh; 01 Jun 1995; Genetics
  140(2):821-841; DOI; PMID; PMC;
  abstract. Fixation probability of a
  favorable mutation is reduced if
  selection at other loci causes
  inherited variation in fitness.
  Substitutions, fluctuating
  polymorphisms, and deleterious
  mutations in a large population. With
  loose linkage r, effects depend on
  additive genetic variance in relative
  fitness var(W) and act by reducing
  effective size: N/Ne = 1 +
  var(W)/2r^2. Tightly linked loci can
  have a substantial effect not
  predictable from Ne. Linked
  deleterious mutations reduce fixation
  probability of weakly favored alleles
  by exp(-2U/R), where U is the total
  mutation rate and R is map length in
  Morgans. Substitutions can cause a
  greater reduction: an allele with
  advantage s < s_crit =
  (pi^2/6) log_e(S/s) [var(W)/R] is
  very unlikely to be fixed. Fluctuating
  polymorphisms at many linked loci can
  also impede fixation. Hitchhiking due
  to all three kinds of selection may
  substantially impede adaptation that
  depends on weakly favored alleles.
  Distinct from filled Hill and
  Robertson 1966 (limits to artificial
  selection under linkage; not this
  fixation-probability construction).
  Distinct from filled Maynard Smith
  and Haigh 1974 hitchhiking. Distinct
  from filled Kaplan-Hudson-Langley
  1989 (sample polymorphism, not
  fixation of a weakly favored
  allele). Distinct from Otto and
  Barton 1997 this wave (recombination
  modifier, not the 1995 s_crit).
  Distinct from filled Barton 1979 /
  1983 / 1986 hybrid-zone papers.
  Barton 2000 Phil Trans is a review
  (not used).
One-sentence prediction: Hitchhiking
  from substitutions, fluctuating
  polymorphisms, and deleterious
  mutations can block fixation of
  weakly favored alleles, especially
  when linkage is tight.
Assumptions: a large sexual population
  with a recombination map and inherited
  fitness variation; a cultivar panel
  is not N; clone IBD is not that
  variation.
Which fail here: a cultivar collection
  is not N. Clone IBD fakes inherited
  fitness variation. Grafted clones
  skip the sexual hitchhike. Dual
  domestication is two origins. Color /
  sex / flavor are few large-effect
  loci, not weakly favored polygenes.
Modern data / statistic: whether
  weakly favored substitutions after
  clone + IBD filters are a Barton-1995
  linkage-limited fixation (s below
  s_crit; exp(-2U/R) from linked
  deleterious mutations), versus clone
  IBD or two origins.
Dataset: Dong et al. 2023 Science
  (DOI 10.1126/science.add8655). Wild
  sylvestris after clone + IBD filters.
  Use as the linkage-limit contrast,
  not as a published grape 1995
  analysis. Do not invent a grape
  s_crit paper.
What would falsify it: weakly favored
  fixation is not a Barton-1995
  linkage limit after clone filter
  (clone IBD, or two origins, or few
  large-effect loci). Artificial-
  selection limits remain Hill-
  Robertson 1966. Classical hitchhiking
  remains Maynard Smith-Haigh 1974.
  Recombination-modifier remains Otto-
  Barton 1997.
What we will not claim: that Hill and
  Robertson 1966 is this paper (already
  filled). That Barton 1979 is this
  paper. That Barton 2000 is the source
  (review). That Dong 2023 estimated
  s_crit. That VviAGL11 is the sex
  locus.
```
